I designed and developed MolBlender’s architecture and its Python API and command-line interface. The toolkit brings together molecular fingerprints, descriptors, sequence and structure embeddings, and other representations from multiple backends.

Its screening workflow compares combinations of representations and models under a common evaluation protocol and stores results for analysis. I use this infrastructure to study how featurization choices affect molecular prediction.
