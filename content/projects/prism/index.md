I contribute to PRISM, a team-developed framework for automating protein–ligand simulation setup and analysis. My work includes the free-energy perturbation workflow and documentation for configuring force-field combinations.

PRISM supports multiple force fields and workflows for molecular dynamics, umbrella sampling, and enhanced sampling.

The PRISM bioRxiv preprint describes the simulation platform. The later ETALON preprint describes how PRISM is used for physics-based modeling in a broader research workflow.
