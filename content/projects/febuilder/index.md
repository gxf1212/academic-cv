I developed FEbuilder to automate a recurring bottleneck in relative binding free-energy calculations. It takes a reference complex and a candidate ligand, constructs a hybrid topology, and prepares NAMD/CHARMM inputs for free-energy perturbation.

I presented FEbuilder at ACS Fall 2024.
