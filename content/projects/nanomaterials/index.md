My graduate research examines how polymer chemistry influences self-assembly, membrane affinity, and cellular uptake.

- **Polymer assembly and membrane interactions.** I use coarse-grained simulation to study assemblies of hyaluronan and zwitterionic polymers. I also combine simulations and molecular machine learning to prioritize polymer motifs for membrane-interaction studies.
- **Functionalized nanodots.** I developed QSAR workflows to compare molecular representations and regression models for cellular uptake and transport data. I used substructure-level attribution to interpret model predictions.

These are ongoing research projects.
