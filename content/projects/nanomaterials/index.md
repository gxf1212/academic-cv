My graduate research examines how polymer chemistry influences self-assembly, membrane affinity, and cellular uptake.

- **Hyaluronan nanogel self-assembly.** I used coarse-grained molecular dynamics to study how hyaluronan and the zwitterionic polymer OPDEA assemble. [Public simulation inputs for related polymer delivery work](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17078485)
- **Polymer–membrane affinity.** I combine molecular dynamics and machine learning to prioritize zwitterionic polymer motifs for membrane-interaction studies. [Public data and analysis code](https://github.com/gxf1212/OP_unit_paper)
- **FIND nanodots.** I developed QSAR models of cellular uptake and transport for fingerprint-implanted nanodots and used substructure-level attribution to interpret the models.
