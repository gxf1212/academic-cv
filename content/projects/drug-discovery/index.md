My work combines virtual screening and molecular dynamics with relative binding free-energy calculations to assess ligand designs.

In the SAGE study, I contributed to a computational workflow for exploring noncovalent CDK7 inhibitors. It combined fragment-guided design, docking, molecular dynamics, and free-energy perturbation to prioritize candidates. I have also applied related methods in projects involving peptide inhibitors.

[SAGE journal article](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpcb.5c02076)
